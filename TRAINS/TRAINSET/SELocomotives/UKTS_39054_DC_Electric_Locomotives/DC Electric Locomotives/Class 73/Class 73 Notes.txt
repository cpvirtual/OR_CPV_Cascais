You will need to adjust MaxForce ( 20000lbf ) so that current is appropriate on ammeter. With automatic throttle current should max about 1700 A.

When working on electric power control resistance flag should show as follows:

For manual throttle all throttle positions above 0% and below 40% and all throttle positions above 50% and below 90%.

(In other words resistance flag only clears at OFF and at running notches 40%-50% and 90%-100% - see driving instructions in eng file.)

For automatic throttle: 

Class 73/0

Notch 1 3% - resistance flag appears at all speeds

Notch 2 40% - resistance flag appears at speeds 0 to 12 mph

Notch 3 90% and Notch 4 100% - resistance flag appears at speeds 0 to 12 mph and 13 to 20 mph

Class 73/1

Notch 1 3% - resistance flag appears at all speeds

Notch 2 40% - resistance flag appears at speeds 0 to 13 mph

Notch 3 90% and Notch 4 100% - resistance flag appears at speeds 0 to 13 mph and 15 to 23 mph
